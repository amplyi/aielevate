Organisations rarely stall because of missing expertise. They stall when strategy, execution and accountability stop aligning—and when unresolved decisions weaken direction, ownership and momentum.

Why this category exists

Organizations do not fail from lack of information. They fail from loss of decision memory.

Most organizations can track transactions, workflows, and outputs. What they cannot reliably preserve is why a decision was made, which assumptions shaped it, how ownership shifted, where intervention should have happened, and what precedent should have been reused.
