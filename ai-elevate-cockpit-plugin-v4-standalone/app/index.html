ABOUT AI ELEVATE

Built for the organisational questions technology alone cannot answer

AI Elevate is an independent, vendor neutral consultancy founded by Anthony van Lobbrecht and shaped by more than 25 years across enterprise technology, implementation, operations, commercial transformation and organisational change.

The proposition did not begin with AI

It developed through years of working where strategy, technology, delivery and organisational reality meet.

Organisations rarely stall because expertise is completely absent. They stall when strategy, execution and accountability stop aligning, when responsibilities become fragmented and when unresolved decisions weaken direction and momentum.

AI is accelerating that challenge. Organisations can introduce tools faster than they can redesign authority, processes, architecture and accountability around them. Adoption advances while organisational capability remains fragmented.

AI Elevate works at that intersection.
